FAQ

Good questions

How is this different from Screen Time? +

Screen Time is easy to ignore — one tap and you're back in. Go To Bed is built to be a real wall at night, with enough friction that the easy choice becomes putting the phone down.

Can I still get to my phone in an emergency? +

Of course. Calls, messages, and anything you don't block stay available. You decide exactly which apps get locked.

Do you collect my data? +

No. There's no account and no tracking — your schedule and app choices stay on your device. See our privacy policy.
